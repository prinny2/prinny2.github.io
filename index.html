LeadBellus

ResonAnza

Brazil's oil regulator publishes records for 31,244 wells. They were collected to find oil. ResonAnza reads them again to find energy, mainly heat.

Visit the site
resonanza.com.br

Water produced by three Potiguar fields, m³ per day

Move over the chart to read a month.

Regulator data. March 2026 was never published, so it stays a gap instead of a fake zero.

The data killed my first plan, so I changed the plan.

I started out to generate electricity from hot wells. Real well logs came back at 24 to 29 °C/km onshore, below the roughly 30 the turbines need. I dropped it. The focus now is direct heat from the water these fields already produce.

Measured: 24 to 29 °C/km
Needed: about 30

Every number in ResonAnza is labeled measured or modeled, and keeps its source.

Extraction pipeline

Python that reads scanned well reports with Claude, corrects bottom-hole temperatures with the Horner method and calibrates per basin.

Service and dashboard

FastAPI, a Typer CLI and a React 19 dashboard, with PDF, GeoJSON and KML exports, on Cloud Run with Postgres.

Tests

450+ pytest tests, with lint and coverage in CI.

Where it stands: a registered company in Brazil at the study stage, with no paying customer or pilot yet.
